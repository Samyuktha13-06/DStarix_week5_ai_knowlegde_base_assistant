# Sample Questions

Here is a list of sample questions you can use to interact with the AI Knowledge Base Assistant:

## 1. General Internship Questions
- **Question:** How long is the internship?
- **Question:** What is the duration of the training program?
- **Question:** When do I receive the internship completion certificate?

## 2. Attendance and Work Schedule
- **Question:** What are the working days?
- **Question:** Do we have to work on weekends?
- **Question:** What are the working hours?
- **Question:** What is the policy for leaves?

## 3. Tooling and AI Policies
- **Question:** Can interns use ChatGPT?
- **Question:** What is the policy regarding AI coding assistants?
- **Question:** Are we allowed to share company code with external tools?

## 4. Evaluation and Deliverables
- **Question:** How will interns be evaluated?
- **Question:** What criteria are used to assess my performance?
- **Question:** What happens if an intern fails to meet expectations?

## 5. Contact Information
- **Question:** Who should I contact for technical questions?
- **Question:** Who is the HR point of contact?
- **Question:** Who is the supervisor?
